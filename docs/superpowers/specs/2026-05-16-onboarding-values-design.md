# Onboarding Values Design

Date: 2026-05-16
Status: Approved for copy and UI planning

## Decision Summary

GDGoC CNU onboarding is for 전남대학교 students who are seeing GDGoC CNU for the first time.

CNU means Chonnam National University, 전남대학교. Public copy must not describe CNU as 충남대학교, Chungnam National University, or 충남대.

The first impression should be:

> GDGoC CNU is a trusted Chonnam National University student developer community connected to the Google Developers ecosystem.

Build with AI is a seasonal campaign and activity example. It should not replace the permanent chapter identity.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- 전남대학교 재학생 who do not yet know GDGoC CNU.
- Students who need to understand the chapter's credibility, values, and activities before deciding whether to join or attend.

Secondary audience:

- Existing event participants who need a clear path into chapter activity.
- Mentors, partners, and organizers who need to understand what the chapter stands for.

## Onboarding Message Architecture

Use this order for the onboarding screen and supporting copy.

1. Identity
   - GDGoC CNU is the 전남대학교 chapter connected to the Google Developers ecosystem.
   - The chapter is a student developer community, not only an event page.

2. Goal
   - Impact students.
   - Empower students to impact their communities through technology.

3. Values
   - Connect
   - Learn
   - Grow

4. Roles
   - Host workshops.
   - Build projects.

5. Benefits
   - Professional growth.
   - Network growth.
   - Community learning.

## Copy Principles

- Korean-first, with official English terms preserved.
- Say "전남대학교" when the university must be explicit.
- Use "CNU" only after the university identity is already clear.
- Keep "Google Developers" visible as a credibility signal.
- Avoid generic AI hype. Tie AI and Google tools to workshops, projects, and learning outcomes.
- Avoid making Build with AI sound like the permanent identity of the chapter.

## Recommended First-Viewport Copy Direction

The first viewport should communicate:

- We are GDGoC CNU, the 전남대학교 student developer community connected to Google Developers.
- We help students connect, learn, and grow.
- We host workshops and build projects so students can gain professional growth, network growth, and community learning.

Possible hero line:

> 전남대학교 학생 개발자가 Google Developers 생태계와 연결되는 커뮤니티

Possible support copy:

> GDGoC CNU는 전남대학교 학생들이 기술로 성장하고, 그 성장을 커뮤니티에 다시 연결하도록 돕습니다. 우리는 워크숍을 열고 프로젝트를 만들며 함께 배우는 개발자 네트워크를 만듭니다.

## UI Implications

- The hero should prioritize chapter identity and trust before campaign-specific content.
- `Connect / Learn / Grow` should be the primary value triad.
- `Workshop / Project` should be presented as the main activity roles.
- `Professional Growth / Network Growth / Community Learning` should appear as benefits or proof points.
- Build with AI can appear as a current campaign strip, poster area, or activity example below the identity/value message.

## Non-Goals

- Do not present GDGoC CNU as a generic AI club.
- Do not present Build with AI as the whole product identity.
- Do not use 충남대, 충남대학교, or Chungnam in public copy.
- Do not over-explain internal implementation details such as Firestore, admin flows, or demo mechanics on the onboarding screen.
